Privacy

Housing Comps keeps a log of how the site is used: sign-ins, searches and search boundaries, records opened, saved searches, contributions and exports. Visitors who aren't signed in are logged under a random browser ID; signed-in activity is tied to the account.

The log is used to run and improve the site and to understand who uses it. It is visible only to the site owner. Your email is never shown publicly, and your information is not sold. Contributions are credited the way you choose.

To see or delete what's stored about you, email the site owner.
