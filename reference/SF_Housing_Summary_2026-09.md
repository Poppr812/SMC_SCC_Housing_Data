# San Francisco housing stock and development activity

Data through 2026 Q2. Compiled September 9, 2026. Companion to `SF_Housing_Stock_and_Pipeline_2026-09.xlsx` (data, formulas, sources, update guide) and `SF_Housing_Brief_2026-09.html` (charts).

## The short version

San Francisco has about 423,000 housing units. It added 2,669 net units in 2025 (2,406 from new construction), up from a decade-low 1,603 in 2024 but still ~26% below the ten-year average of 3,622. Two-thirds of 2025's new units were deed-restricted affordable. The first half of 2026 delivered only 405 units.

The pipeline shows 75,853 units, but half of that is the remaining phases of 14 master plans, and another 22,500 units are approved or under review with no building permit. Units actually under construction total 3,301, down from 4,545 five years ago. The near-term signal (under construction + issued + approved permits) is 6,942 units.

## Project tracking (workbook Projects tab, 265 rows)

Coverage: every project of 10+ units in the 2023–2025 Housing Inventory appendices (completed, approved, authorized, filed), the MOHCD/OCII Affordable Housing Pipeline (~170 projects with sponsor, AMI mix, schedule), named market-rate projects, and the 14 multi-phase remainders. Small projects and ADUs are not itemized; the DataSF pipeline export covers them.

Tracked units by status: Complete (2023–2026 YTD) 6,022 in 44 projects; Under Construction 3,544 in 35; Permitted (site permit, no FCD) 3,127 in 36; Approved 29,126 in 100; Under Review 9,229 in 36; Multi-phase remaining 40,479.

By size bucket (all statuses): ≤4 units 0; 5–10 units 23; 11–20 units 314; 21–50 units 1,776; 51–250 units 15,862; 251+ units 73,552. Almost all tracked volume is in buildings over 50 units, and the 251+ bucket is dominated by master plans and SoMa towers.

## Caveats

- Housing Inventory counts TCO'd units as complete; phased TCOs can split a project across two years.
- "Under construction" in the Pipeline Report means authorized to build, not verified activity. MOHCD "Site Work Permit Issued" (status 4) is treated here as Permitted, not under construction.
- Capital-stack fields are populated only where CTCAC/CDLAC, MOHCD Loan Committee, CalHFA, or press records exist; market-rate lenders are rarely disclosed. Blank means not found.
- The ACS age and tenure figures are 5-year estimates from secondary compilations; refresh from data.census.gov (DP04) for a citable series.
